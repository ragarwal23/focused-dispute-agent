# Refunds

**Agarwal Motor Finance · Servicing Policy RF · Version 1 · Effective January 1, 2025**

This policy covers what we refund, how and when refunds go out, and how to answer a customer asking where their refund is. "Business day" means Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays.

## RF.v1.s1 — What we refund

- Overpayments, including a second payment for the same installment.
- A late fee that was waived after the customer had already paid it.
- A credit balance left after the loan is paid off.

Every refund starts with a refund request recorded on the account.

## RF.v1.s2 — How and when refunds are issued

Refunds are issued within **10 business days** of the request. We send refunds by ACH to the bank account on file. If there is no bank account on file, or the customer asks for a check, we mail a check to the address on file.

ACH refunds usually reach the customer's bank 1–3 business days after they are issued, and each carries a trace number the customer's bank can look up. Allow 7–10 business days for a mailed check to arrive after it is issued.

## RF.v1.s3 — Checking a refund's status

A refund has been issued only when the account shows an issued refund with its date, its method and, for ACH, its trace number. An account note saying a refund was sent is not proof that it was issued.

When a refund has been issued, give the customer the issue date, the method, the last four digits of the bank account it was sent to, and the trace number.

## RF.v1.s4 — Refunds that are late or in doubt

Refer the case to a servicing supervisor when:

- no refund has been issued 10 business days after the request;
- an account note says a refund was sent, but no issued refund is on the account.

## RF.v1.s5 — Overpayments we cannot see

If the customer says they paid twice but only one payment is on the account, ask for a bank statement showing both payments leaving their account. Do not open a refund request until the second payment is found.

## RF.v1.s6 — More than one open refund request

If more than one refund request is open and the customer has not said which one they mean, confirm the amount or the reason with them before giving a status or referring the case.
