# Payment Extensions

**Agarwal Motor Finance · Collections Policy EX · Version 1 · Effective January 1, 2025**

A payment extension moves one monthly installment to the end of the loan term for a customer facing a short-term hardship. Extensions are approved by Collections, not by servicing representatives.

## EX.v1.s1 — What an extension does

An approved extension moves the next installment to the end of the loan and extends the maturity date by one month. Interest continues to accrue during the extended month.

## EX.v1.s2 — Eligibility

The loan must have been open for at least 6 months, with at least 6 payments made. A customer can have one extension in any 12-month period and no more than three over the life of the loan. The account cannot be more than 30 days past due when the request is made.

## EX.v1.s3 — Fees

There is no fee for an extension. If an extension is approved before an installment's due date, no late fee applies to that installment. An extension does not remove a late fee that was already assessed.

## EX.v1.s4 — How customers request one

Customers request an extension online or by phone. Servicing representatives transfer the call to Collections and do not promise approval.
