# Returned and Reversed Payments

**Agarwal Motor Finance · Servicing Policy RR · Version 1 · Effective January 1, 2025**

A **returned** payment is one the customer's bank sends back to us. A **reversal** is one we make ourselves, to correct a payment applied to the wrong loan or applied twice. "Business day" means Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays.

## RR.v1.s1 — Return reason codes

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| R01 | Insufficient funds |
| R02 | Account closed |
| R08 | Payment stopped at the account holder's request |
| R10 | Account holder says the payment was not authorized |

A return can arrive several business days after the payment first appeared in the customer's bank account. A debit that showed in the customer's account, followed by a return, is normal and does not mean either record is wrong.

## RR.v1.s2 — Returned payment fee

A returned payment fee of **$15** is assessed when the return posts. It is due with the replacement payment.

## RR.v1.s3 — After a return

The returned payment is treated as never received, and the customer must make a replacement payment. If the replacement arrives by the end of the grace period for that installment, no late fee applies. If it does not, the late fee under LF.v3.s4 applies in addition to the returned payment fee.

## RR.v1.s4 — When the customer disputes a return

Only the customer's bank can confirm why it returned a payment. If the customer says the return was the bank's mistake, ask for a letter from the bank, or a statement showing the funds were available on the payment date. The returned payment fee stays in place until the bank confirms its error.

## RR.v1.s5 — Reversals

Payments Operations reverses a payment when it was applied to the wrong loan or applied twice. Only Payments Operations enters reversals, and every reversal is noted on the account.

## RR.v1.s6 — Payments applied to another customer's loan

When a payment was applied to the wrong loan, Payments Operations reverses it from that loan and applies it to the correct one with its original received date. Representatives must not move payments or share any information about another customer's account. Refer the case to a servicing supervisor.

## RR.v1.s7 — Reversals made in error

If a reversal was entered in error, the original payment is valid. Payments Operations re-applies it with its original received date. Any late fee caused by the reversal is eligible for fee adjustment review under LF.v3.s5.

## RR.v1.s8 — When to refer to a servicing supervisor

Refer the case to a servicing supervisor when:

- account notes conflict with the account records, or with what the customer says;
- the evidence involves another customer's account.
