# Payments, Grace Period and Late Fees

**Agarwal Motor Finance · Servicing Policy LF · Version 3 · Effective May 1, 2026**

This policy tells servicing representatives when a monthly installment is due, when a payment counts as received, and when a late fee applies. It replaces version 2. "Business day" means Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays. All times are lender local time.

## LF.v3.s1 — Due date and grace period

Each installment is due on the due day shown on the customer's contract. The customer then has a grace period of 10 calendar days. The grace period ends at 11:59 p.m. on the 10th day after the due date. For example, if the due day is the 3rd, the grace period ends at 11:59 p.m. on the 13th.

The grace period is not extended when its last day falls on a weekend or holiday.

## LF.v3.s2 — When a payment counts as received

A payment counts toward the grace period on the date and time we **receive** it, not the date it posts to the account. Posting can take up to two business days after we receive a payment.

| How the customer pays | When we receive it |
|---|---|
| AutoPay | When the scheduled draft runs on the due date |
| Online (website or app) | When the customer submits the payment |
| Phone (with a representative or the automated line) | When the payment is submitted on the call |
| Mailed check | When the check arrives at our payment processing center |
| Bank bill pay | When the payment arrives at our payment processing center. Most bill-pay services mail a paper check, so allow 5–7 business days. The date the customer's bank sends it does not count |

## LF.v3.s3 — Payments we cannot apply right away

A check that arrives without a payment coupon or an account number is held in unapplied funds while Payments Operations identifies the account. Once the account is identified, the payment is applied with the date we received it. A customer whose payment was held this way paid on the date we received it.

## LF.v3.s4 — Late fee

If an installment has not been received by the end of the grace period, a late fee of **$25** is assessed at 12:05 a.m. on the day after the grace period ends. Only one late fee is assessed per installment.

A payment that the customer's bank later returns does not count as received (see RR.v1.s3).

## LF.v3.s5 — Fees caused by our error

A late fee caused by a failure on our side is eligible for **fee adjustment review**. Failures on our side include:

- a processing outage that stopped a payment or AutoPay draft from running;
- a payment received in time but applied to the wrong account or held in unapplied funds;
- a payment received in time but posted after the fee was assessed;
- a keying error, such as a reversal entered in error.

Submit these for fee adjustment review with the records that show the error. Representatives do not remove fees themselves.

## LF.v3.s6 — The customer says they paid, but we have no record

Search the account by date, amount and confirmation number. If the payment is not on the account, ask the customer for proof before taking further action: the payment date, the amount, the payee name, the confirmation number, and a bank statement showing the payment left their account.

Confirmation numbers can be partial or can match payments on other customers' accounts. Never share another customer's account information. If a payment appears to have been applied to another customer's loan, refer the case to a servicing supervisor. Payments Operations will research and move the payment (see RR.v1.s6).

## LF.v3.s7 — Courtesy waivers

A representative may grant one courtesy waiver of a late fee in any 12-month period. A courtesy waiver is a goodwill gesture, not a finding that the fee was wrong. If the customer already paid the waived fee, open a refund request for the fee amount (see RF.v1.s1).

## LF.v3.s8 — When to refer to a servicing supervisor

Refer the case to a servicing supervisor when:

- account notes conflict with the account records, or with what the customer says;
- the customer says a representative gave them incorrect information about a payment or about AutoPay;
- the evidence involves another customer's account.
