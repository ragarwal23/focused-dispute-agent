# AutoPay

**Agarwal Motor Finance · Servicing Policy AP · Version 1 · Effective January 1, 2025**

AutoPay drafts the monthly installment from the customer's bank account automatically. This policy covers enrolling, how drafts work, cancelling, and what happens when a draft does not run. "Business day" means Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays.

## AP.v1.s1 — Enrolling

Customers can enroll in AutoPay online, by phone, or when they sign their contract. AutoPay drafts from the bank account on file for the loan.

## AP.v1.s2 — When the draft runs

The draft runs on the due date each month for the installment amount. The payment counts as received when the draft runs (see LF.v3.s2). If the customer's bank later returns the draft, see RR.v1.s3.

## AP.v1.s3 — Paying manually while enrolled

A manual payment does not cancel or skip that month's draft. If the customer pays manually and the draft also runs, the second payment is an overpayment, and the customer may request a refund (see RF.v1.s1). To avoid a double payment, the customer must ask us to skip the draft at least 3 business days before the due date.

## AP.v1.s4 — Cancelling AutoPay

Customers can cancel online, by phone, or through the automated phone line. A cancellation made at least 3 business days before the next draft stops that draft and every draft after it.

Once AutoPay is cancelled, the customer must pay each installment manually. If no payment is received by the end of the grace period, the late fee under LF.v3.s4 applies and is valid. That AutoPay was active in an earlier month does not change this.

## AP.v1.s5 — Drafts that fail on our side

If a scheduled draft does not run because of a processing problem on our side, the customer is not at fault. Any late fee that follows is eligible for fee adjustment review under LF.v3.s5. Tell the customer that the draft did not run and that the fee is being reviewed.

## AP.v1.s6 — Confirming whether AutoPay was active

The account's enrollment and cancellation records show whether AutoPay was active on a given date. If an account note says a representative told the customer something different, refer the case to a servicing supervisor (see LF.v3.s8).
