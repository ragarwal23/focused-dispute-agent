# Payments, Grace Period and Late Fees

**Agarwal Motor Finance · Servicing Policy LF · Version 2 · Effective January 1, 2024**

This policy tells servicing representatives when a monthly installment is due, when a payment is credited, and when a late fee applies. "Business day" means Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays.

## LF.v2.s1 — Due date and grace period

Each installment is due on the due day shown on the customer's contract. The customer has a grace period of 7 calendar days after the due date.

## LF.v2.s2 — When a payment is credited

A payment is credited on the date it posts to the account. Payments by mail or bank bill pay should be sent at least 7 business days before the due date to allow for mail and processing time.

## LF.v2.s3 — Late fee

If an installment has not been credited by the end of the grace period, a late fee of **$30** is assessed the next day.

## LF.v2.s4 — Fee waivers

A servicing supervisor may waive a late fee at their discretion. Representatives may not waive fees.

## LF.v2.s5 — Payments we have no record of

Ask the customer to send a copy of the cancelled check or a bank statement. Do not discuss the payment further until proof is received.
